METHODOLOGY // 02

Less, but better.

My approach to product management and engineering is grounded in the belief that design elevates the essential functions of a product. I adhere to the ten principles of good design.

01

Is innovative

Imaginative design develops in tandem with improving technology, and can never be an end in itself.

02

Makes a product useful

Good design emphasizes usefulness whilst disregarding anything that could detract from it.

03

Is aesthetic

The aesthetic quality is integral to usefulness. Only well-executed objects can be beautiful.

04

Makes a product understandable

It clarifies structure and makes the product clearly express its function using user intuition.

05

Is unobtrusive

Products are like tools. Their design should be neutral and restrained to leave room for the user.

06

Is honest

It does not attempt to manipulate the consumer with promises that cannot be kept.

07

Is long-lasting

It avoids being fashionable and therefore never appears antiquated, lasting many years.

08

Is thorough down to the last detail

Nothing must be arbitrary. Care and accuracy in the design process show respect to the consumer.

09

Is environmentally friendly

It conserves resources and minimizes physical and visual pollution throughout the lifecycle.

10

Is as little design as possible

Simple as possible but not simpler. Concentrating on the essential aspects.
